Instructions

1

Boil pasta al dente

Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the fettuccine and cook according to package instructions until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup of pasta water before draining.

10:00 Pasta Boiling
2

Sauté garlic

While pasta boils, heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-low heat. Add minced garlic and sauté for 1-2 minutes until fragrant but not browned.

3

Whisk sauce

Slowly whisk in the heavy cream and bring to a gentle simmer. Gradually add the grated parmesan cheese, whisking constantly until smooth and creamy.
